# Brand

**Marcello Studio · New York · Creative technology for fashion.**

- Fashion first.
- Technology can be invisible.
- Avoid generic SaaS aesthetics.
- Avoid generic AI aesthetics.
- Interfaces should feel editorial, intentional, and culturally aware.
- Marcello should be credible alongside excellent designers, ateliers, and fashion houses.
- Brand desirability is a strategic asset.
- Marcello Studio remains the simple public master identity.

Apply these principles to concepts, language, and interfaces. Do not invent a new typography, color, or design system as part of repository organization. The existing website remains the implementation reference, not a mandate to redesign it.

Use the [brand review procedure](../skills/brand-review.md) for specific critiques.

## Remote-fitting experience

**Technology backstage. Craftsmanship frontstage.** The customer should feel that their tailor knows them, not that an AI scanned them. The human tailor owns the appointment; convenience should feel like an upgrade. Interfaces should be beautiful, restrained, personal and fashion-native, with editorial hierarchy, strong typography, negative space, calm interaction and minimal chrome. Avoid clinical/body-scanning, generic SaaS/AI and default dashboard aesthetics.

The current internal capture tool serves Harry/Nate and validation. Final luxury packaging and customer rituals are future opportunities. Christian’s existing Marcello and Haitch design work informs taste; do not copy Haitch or redesign the production site.
