# Growth weekly review

## Input

This week’s evidence, prior baseline, opportunities, press/contact references, experiments and current fitting milestone.

## Procedure

1. Review changes in qualified demand, appointments, commissions/revenue and cultural value; preserve missing/unknown data. Do not substitute likes/follower growth for business outcomes.
2. Reuse existing radar/research output; update only changed records, deduplicate opportunities and close expired ones with reasons.
3. Review approved experiment results and attribution limits; separate completed actions, plans and learning. Run [attribution-review](attribution-review.md) when new commission evidence warrants it.
4. Produce at most three recommended actions across all growth channels—or NO ACTION REQUIRED TODAY. Include evidence, effort and approval needs; avoid routine reporting chores when nothing changed.
5. Hand the concise result to the Chief of Staff for the existing [weekly review](weekly-review.md). Do not assign work directly to Christian or launch outreach, spending or website changes.

## Output

- What materially changed and what was learned
- Qualified demand/commercial evidence with sources and gaps
- Maximum three proposed actions or no action
- Decisions needing prioritization, not a separate founder task list

Run manually under the [recommended cadence](../company/AGENT_CADENCE.md). Distinguish facts, assumptions and unknowns; link evidence. Follow [root instructions](../AGENTS.md).
