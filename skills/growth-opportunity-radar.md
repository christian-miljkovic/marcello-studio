# Growth opportunity radar

## Input

Current milestone, [baseline](../growth/HAITCH_BASELINE.md), [opportunities](../growth/OPPORTUNITIES.md), available research sources and known brand constraints.

## Procedure

1. Review only new or materially changed evidence across press, search, AI discovery, partnerships, stylists, wedding planners, cultural collaborators and geographic demand. Do not create a task for every channel.
2. Verify promising sources and dates; distinguish evidence, inference and unknowns. Use [pr-research](pr-research.md) for a real editorial opportunity rather than mass pitch generation.
3. Assess RELEVANCE, REACH, CONVERSION POTENTIAL, CULTURAL VALUE, TIMING and HUMAN EFFORT using the qualitative framework; do not calculate a fake-precision score or invent reach.
4. Reject marketing sludge and poor brand fit; defer work that displaces fitting validation without a deliberate prioritization decision. Deduplicate previous ideas and respect existing relationship ownership.
5. Update the opportunity record with source, rationale, effort and approval needs. Submit no more than three recommended actions total to the Chief of Staff, or exactly NO ACTION REQUIRED TODAY. Do not send, publish, spend or change production.

## Output

- Maximum three recommendations across all channels, each with evidence and tradeoffs
- Timing, human effort, smallest next step and approval needed
- Or: NO ACTION REQUIRED TODAY.

Run manually under the [recommended cadence](../company/AGENT_CADENCE.md). Distinguish facts, assumptions and unknowns; link evidence. Follow [root instructions](../AGENTS.md).
