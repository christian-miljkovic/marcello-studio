# Growth & Distribution

**The brand creates culture. Marcello amplifies it.** HAITCH is the first laboratory for a secondary Client Work capability: software, AI-assisted research, data, search/AEO, PR, attribution and distribution that protect fashion credibility. Remote fitting validation remains the primary Marcello Work priority.

These are lightweight internal records for [Growth & Distribution](../agents/growth-distribution.md), invoked manually through Codex. No CRM application, background monitoring or external integrations are enabled.

Manual invocation: “Use agents/growth-distribution.md and skills/growth-weekly-review.md. Review the supplied evidence, update internal records and send at most three recommendations to the Chief of Staff. Draft only; do not contact anyone.” A manual export is enough to start; missing connections are reported once, not recreated as daily chores.

Optimize for qualified demand, appointments, revenue and cultural value. An enquiry is not a booked appointment, a pilot request is not willingness to pay, and an impression is not a commission. Counts without a source/period stay UNKNOWN, not zero. “NO ACTION REQUIRED TODAY.” is valid.

Research and drafting are allowed internally. Christian’s specific approval is required before outreach, publishing, spending, public claims or production changes. Chief of Staff ranking does not grant that permission. [Brand review](../skills/brand-review.md) applies to proposed customer-facing creative work. Keep personal customer data, private messages and contact details in approved restricted storage; repository artifacts should use aggregate data or non-sensitive references.
