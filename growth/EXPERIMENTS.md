# Growth experiments

No growth experiments approved or running in this record. These are secondary Client Work experiments; the product’s remote-fitting roadmap is unchanged.

## Record template

- Experiment ID and linked opportunity; client/project; owner.
- Question and falsifiable hypothesis; facts versus assumptions.
- Audience/channel and smallest intervention; brand-review considerations.
- Baseline period/source, primary outcome and denominator (qualified enquiries, confirmed appointments, commissions/revenue, or a justified cultural outcome).
- Observation window, sample limitations, success/stop criteria chosen before execution; UNKNOWN thresholds must be resolved before launch.
- Effort and explicit budget ceiling, including zero; dependencies and instrumentation gaps.
- Christian approval reference, exact permitted action/audience/spend/production scope and expiry if given. Internal ranking is not launch approval.
- Status: DRAFT / AWAITING_APPROVAL / APPROVED / RUNNING / COMPLETE / STOPPED.
- Actual execution dates, evidence, cost, results and attribution confidence.
- Decision: continue, revise, stop or inconclusive; concise learning for the Chief of Staff.

No default ad spending, public posting, email sending or site modification. Keep drafts internal. Report weak or inconclusive evidence honestly; a before/after change alone does not prove causation. Do not generate more content to satisfy a posting cadence.
