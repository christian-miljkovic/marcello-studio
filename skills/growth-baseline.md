# Growth baseline

## Input

HAITCH/Marcello scope, date range/timezone, authorized exports or supplied evidence and [HAITCH baseline](../growth/HAITCH_BASELINE.md). Follow [Growth & Distribution](../agents/growth-distribution.md).

## Procedure

1. Inventory actual evidence and permissions; use supplied exports first. Do not create connections, subscriptions or instrumentation. Record missing sources once.
2. Capture website activity with the source’s exact definitions, geography, traffic sources, enquiries, confirmed appointments, fitting preference, Remote Atelier requests, search/AI visibility, coverage and attributed revenue where known.
3. Record source, period, filters, collection date and coverage for every measure. Do not equate visitors with sessions, enquiries with appointments, requests with paid demand or UNKNOWN with zero.
4. Preserve raw preference values and apply the baseline’s normalized mapping. Show unrecorded preferences and approximate geography limitations separately.
5. Choose a comparable prior period only where definitions and coverage match. For AI answers, record query, engine/model if exposed, date, locale, answer/citation evidence and sampling limits; do not manufacture a visibility score.
6. Update aggregate internal records and send material findings/gaps to the Chief of Staff. Keep PII and restricted exports outside Git.

## Output

- Dated baseline with sources and definitions
- Real changes versus coverage changes
- Missing instrumentation/access clearly labeled
- At most three justified recommendations; no action is acceptable

Run manually under the [recommended cadence](../company/AGENT_CADENCE.md). Distinguish facts, assumptions and unknowns; link evidence. Follow [root instructions](../AGENTS.md).
