# Growth experiment

## Input

A prioritized opportunity, baseline, budget/effort constraints and brand context. Use [experiment record](../growth/EXPERIMENTS.md).

## Procedure

1. State one question and hypothesis tied to qualified demand, appointments, revenue or justified cultural value. Separate facts from assumptions.
2. Define the smallest test, audience/channel, measurement source, observation window, success/stop criteria and sample limitations before execution. Resolve material unknown thresholds before launching.
3. Specify human effort, budget ceiling and exact external actions. Do not assume source attribution or existing instrumentation covers the test.
4. Get Creative Director review where customer-facing creative is involved. Prepare a reviewable proposal for the Chief of Staff; Christian must separately approve outreach, publication, spending or production changes.
5. Record actual approval scope/date. This skill designs the experiment; execute external actions only in an explicitly authorized task.
6. When results are supplied, compare to predeclared criteria, record actual cost and limitations, and recommend continue/revise/stop/inconclusive. Update internal evidence without expanding the product roadmap.

## Output

- Completed experiment proposal with hypothesis and test criteria
- Brand/measurement risks, budget/effort and approval scope
- On review: evidence, result, uncertainty and one decision for Chief of Staff

Run manually under the [recommended cadence](../company/AGENT_CADENCE.md). Distinguish facts, assumptions and unknowns; link evidence. Follow [root instructions](../AGENTS.md).
