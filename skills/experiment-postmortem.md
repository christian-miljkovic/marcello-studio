# Experiment postmortem

## Input

The experiment plan, recorded measurements, and artifacts.

## Procedure

Compare actual evidence to the predeclared criteria. Separate result from interpretation; include failures, inconclusive outcomes, and deviations. Link evidence, update supported assumptions in the spec, and choose the smallest next test.

## Output

- Hypothesis
- Result
- Evidence
- Interpretation
- What changed
- Next experiment

Distinguish facts, assumptions, and hypotheses. Link evidence; mark unanswered fields `TBD`. Follow [root instructions](../AGENTS.md).
