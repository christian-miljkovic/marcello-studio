# PR research

## Input

One shortlisted opportunity, brand context, known relationship history and current public sources. Follow [Growth & Distribution](../agents/growth-distribution.md).

## Procedure

1. Verify the journalist’s current outlet and beat; read recent relevant articles and retain titles, URLs and dates. Do not rely on remembered affiliations or snippets alone.
2. Explain why HAITCH/Marcello is relevant to that coverage, a specific story angle and a real timing reason. Flag missing evidence, imagery, quotes, public-claim approval or unresolved manufacturing facts.
3. Check the supplied contact history and relationship owner; unknown last contact stays UNKNOWN, not “never contacted.” Do not infer personal addresses or scrape a bulk list.
4. Update [PRESS_RADAR](../growth/PRESS_RADAR.md) and a minimal [CONTACTS](../growth/CONTACTS.md) reference. Keep private correspondence/contact data in restricted storage.
5. Draft a tailored pitch only if useful, with unsupported statements marked for removal/confirmation. Present the concrete recipient/draft to Christian before outreach; no sending or press-release publication. Humans maintain the relationship.

## Output

- Journalist, outlet, beat and recent relevant articles
- Relevance, story angle, timing reason, evidence/assets needed
- Last contact if known and relationship owner
- Optional internal draft; explicit approval still required to contact

Run manually under the [recommended cadence](../company/AGENT_CADENCE.md). Distinguish facts, assumptions and unknowns; link evidence. Follow [root instructions](../AGENTS.md).
