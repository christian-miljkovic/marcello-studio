# Brand review

## Input

A specific interface, concept, or piece of copy plus company/BRAND.md and company/ORGANIZATION.md.

## Procedure

Assess whether the work feels fashion-first, editorial, intentional, culturally credible, and beautifully designed. Look for generic SaaS/AI conventions, unnecessary technology exposure, and loss of the human relationship. Preserve Marcello Studio as the master identity. Ground critiques in specific details, not invented typography or color rules.

## Output

- Overall fit with Marcello positioning
- Specific strengths
- Specific mismatches and rationale
- Smallest recommended revisions
- Open questions

Distinguish facts, assumptions, and hypotheses. Link evidence; mark unanswered fields `TBD`. Follow [root instructions](../AGENTS.md).
