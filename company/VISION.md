# Vision

Marcello Studio is a New York creative technology studio for fashion.

Technology should be culturally desirable, beautifully designed, experimental, and fashion-native. Marcello should feel credible alongside designers and craftspeople, rather than like a generic `.ai` company or enterprise SaaS vendor.

Technology should augment craftspeople and designers without unnecessarily removing the human relationship. Taste, trust, and the tailor's judgment belong in the experience.

The long-term ambition extends beyond tailoring. The current wedge is remote fitting for premium made-to-measure and bespoke clothing, with Haitch as the initial design partner.

Our current milestone is to produce a garment for a customer whom Nate/Harry have never physically measured, with corrections no greater than those expected during a normal first fitting. The [roadmap](../product/ROADMAP.md) serves that milestone; [future opportunities](FUTURE_OPPORTUNITIES.md) are possibilities, not commitments.

For the first 2–3 weeks, the mission is to collect high-quality paired fitting data, observe the complete Haitch workflow, benchmark existing measurement technology, and identify exactly what Marcello needs to invent. Christian is planned Test Subject #001. This is validation, not a complete remote-tailoring product build. Reported customer pain and bespoke as a potential first wedge do not establish demand or commercial viability. See the [source-backed discovery](../customers/haitch/interviews/2026-09-22-discovery-synthesis.md).
