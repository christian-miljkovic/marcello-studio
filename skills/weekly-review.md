# Weekly review

## Input

The week’s evidence, decisions, experiment records, roadmap, and backlog.

## Procedure

Triage the [company inbox](../company/INBOX.md): verify captures, route useful content to decisions, roadmap, future opportunities, research, or the fitting spec, and delete disposable notes or archive historical context. Keep unresolved captures explicitly unprocessed and unverified; examples are not facts. Compare activity to learning and milestone progress. Separate completed work from plans. Revise assumptions only with evidence and record meaningful decisions; choose one objective for next week.

## Output

- What happened
- What was learned
- Changes to assumptions
- Experiment status
- Current bottleneck
- Next week's objective

Distinguish facts, assumptions, and hypotheses. Link evidence; mark unanswered fields `TBD`. Follow [root instructions](../AGENTS.md).


Include new Finance Ops reconciliation/close exceptions and the Growth weekly review in this same review, following the [manual cadence](../company/AGENT_CADENCE.md). Reuse existing reports; do not rerun research just to fill a status update. Consolidate to at most three company priorities and one next-week objective, with remote-fitting validation first. Send CPA questions through Christian and preserve separate approval for any external action.
