# Inbox

A temporary bridge between conversations with another agent and structured company context. Unprocessed entries are **unverified**, not facts, decisions, or roadmap commitments. Keep examples and hypotheses distinct from sourced findings.

## Capture and triage

1. Add a short note under Capture. Source and date are optional; include them when available. Label quotations, assumptions, and questions distinctly.
2. During a priorities or weekly review, check the original context and verify claims before promoting them. Preserve unresolved answers as `TBD`.
3. Route useful material to its durable home:
   - Confirmed company decisions → [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md), appended with date and rationale.
   - Evidence-backed current work → [ROADMAP.md](../product/ROADMAP.md); keep it sparse.
   - Optional future ideas → [FUTURE_OPPORTUNITIES.md](FUTURE_OPPORTUNITIES.md).
   - Sourced findings and open research questions → [research/](../research/README.md); customer-specific evidence → [Haitch research](../customers/haitch/research/README.md).
   - Supported fitting requirements → [REMOTE_FITTING_SPEC.md](../product/REMOTE_FITTING_SPEC.md), with source references.
   - Duplicates or disposable notes → delete; historical context worth retaining → archive with provenance before removing the capture.
4. Remove processed captures after preserving useful content and references. If verification or a decision is still needed, leave the entry explicitly unprocessed and unverified.

Triage is manual. Capturing a note does not authorize implementation or scheduling.
