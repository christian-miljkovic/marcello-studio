# Contacts

A lightweight relationship index, not a CRM app or mailing list. No contacts are seeded. Use public professional information only here; keep private contact details, communications and customer data outside Git in restricted storage.

## Record template

Contact ID; public name; organization; role/beat; type (JOURNALIST / STYLIST / PLANNER / COLLABORATOR / PARTNER); public professional profile/source URL; last verified date; relevance; relationship owner; private record reference if authorized; last contact date/outcome if known; linked opportunity/radar IDs; status; next step and approval reference.

Unknown prior contact remains UNKNOWN. Check with the relationship owner before duplicating outreach. Record do-not-contact preferences and honor them. Do not scrape personal details, infer email addresses or assemble a bulk mailing list. A contact row grants no sending permission. No follow-up sequence or scheduled outreach is enabled.
