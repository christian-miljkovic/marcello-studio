# Organization

```text
                 MARCELLO STUDIO
                    New York

                        │

        Creative technology for fashion

        ┌───────────────┴──────────────┐

     CLIENT WORK                  MARCELLO WORK
                                          │
  Haitch website              Remote fitting system
  custom commerce             experiments
  digital experiences         tools
  embedded engineering        installations
                              collaborations
```

**CLIENT WORK:** Work Marcello performs directly for fashion clients.

**MARCELLO WORK:** Marcello-owned research, products, experiments, tools, installations, and collaborations. Remote fitting is the current priority; the other possibilities are not active roadmap commitments.

Marcello Studio is the master public identity. Do not introduce “Marcello Systems,” “Marcello Labs,” or “Marcello Research” as official brands. Those may remain future organizational concepts; this is the canonical public structure.


## Internal operating roles

These are specialist internal functions, not public Marcello sub-brands. Christian invokes them manually through Codex; the [cadence](AGENT_CADENCE.md) is recommended, not automated.

| Role | Accountability |
| --- | --- |
| [Chief of Staff](../agents/chief-of-staff.md) | Top-level prioritization; consolidates evidence into at most three company priorities |
| [Creative Director](../agents/creative-director.md) | Fashion identity, desirability and creative quality |
| [Product Researcher](../agents/product-researcher.md) | Customer evidence, requirements and unknowns |
| [Technical Lead](../agents/technical-lead.md) | Smallest decisive technical experiment and architecture |
| [Engineer](../agents/engineer.md) | Minimum implementation and verification for approved experiments |
| [Finance Ops](../agents/finance-ops.md) | Source-backed bookkeeping, reconciliation and preparation for a licensed CPA |
| [Growth & Distribution](../agents/growth-distribution.md) | Qualified discovery, appointments, revenue and cultural value while protecting brand desirability |

Finance and Growth submit evidence to the Chief of Staff. They do not create independent work streams for Christian or displace remote-fitting validation. Chief of Staff prioritization does not authorize external action; Christian approves outreach, publishing, spending, public claims and production changes. Final tax/accounting decisions remain with Christian and a licensed CPA.

HAITCH is the first laboratory for the secondary Client Work capability of brand-respecting search/AEO, PR, data, attribution and distribution. This does not expand the active remote-fitting product roadmap or change the public Client Work / Marcello Work structure.
