# Opportunities

No researched opportunities recorded yet. This is not a commitment list. [Growth](../agents/growth-distribution.md) submits at most three recommended actions **across all channels combined** to the Chief of Staff; often the right result is **NO ACTION REQUIRED TODAY.**

## Record template

- Opportunity ID, client/project, date, channel and concise description.
- Evidence URLs, publication/observation dates, verified facts and unknowns.
- Audience fit, proposed action and learning/value expected.
- Relevance: HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW / UNKNOWN, with reason.
- Reach: HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW / UNKNOWN; distinguish relevant audience from raw size; no invented audience counts.
- Conversion potential: HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW / UNKNOWN, with evidence or explicit hypothesis.
- Cultural value: HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW / UNKNOWN, including possible brand harm.
- Timing: NOW / LATER / EXPIRED / UNKNOWN, with actual deadline/source if any.
- Human effort: LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH / UNKNOWN, with concrete time/cost assumptions. Higher effort is a cost, not a positive score.
- Recommendation and rationale; dependencies, required approval, next review trigger.
- Status: RESEARCH / SHORTLIST / DEFERRED / APPROVED / DECLINED / COMPLETED; approval reference and approved scope where present.

No numeric composite or false precision. Prioritize brand fit and plausible qualified demand, then timing and effort. High reach cannot rescue poor cultural fit. Consider press, search, AI discovery, partnerships, stylists, wedding planners, cultural collaborators and geographic demand without requiring one action per channel. Defer anything that displaces the fitting milestone without a deliberate Chief of Staff decision.

Maintain a short evidence trail when merging duplicates or closing expired opportunities; do not repeatedly surface unchanged rejected ideas.
