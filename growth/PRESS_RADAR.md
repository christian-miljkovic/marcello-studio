# Press radar

No journalist research or coverage census has been performed for this setup. No pitches have been sent. Public source links may be recorded here; private correspondence and unpublished contact details stay in approved restricted storage.

## Research record template

- Radar ID, date researched, journalist, outlet and current beat.
- Recent relevant article(s): title, URL, publication date and why relevant; verify current outlet/role rather than rely on memory.
- Why HAITCH/Marcello belongs in this journalist’s coverage; source-backed facts only.
- Specific story angle, evidence/assets needed and unresolved claim checks.
- Timing reason: genuine editorial context/deadline with source, or UNKNOWN.
- Linked [contact reference](CONTACTS.md), relationship owner, last contact date/outcome if known; UNKNOWN is not “never contacted”.
- Brand fit, likely audience relevance, human effort and proposed next step.
- Status: RESEARCH / DEFERRED / DRAFT_READY / APPROVED_TO_CONTACT / CONTACTED / CLOSED; approval/evidence reference for any external action.

Do not mass-generate pitches or infer personal email addresses. AI researches and drafts; humans maintain the relationship. Any outreach requires Christian’s approval of recipient and finished draft. Rank selected opportunities in [OPPORTUNITIES](OPPORTUNITIES.md), subject to its three-action cap.
